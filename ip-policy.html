    The Full Notice

    Terms of this notice.

    1. Exclusive Ownership of Pre-Existing Intellectual Property

    All trademarks, service marks, trade names, logos, proprietary curricula, professional development frameworks, methodologies, digital platforms, lesson structures, and associated intellectual property developed, owned, or utilized by Tiffany A. Davidson remain her exclusive, private property. These assets were established prior to and entirely independent of any external contract, employment, partnership, or institutional engagement.

    2. Non-Transferability via Public, Private, or Governmental Contracts

    Execution of, participation in, or delivery of services under any state government contract, public or private school employment agreement, charter school contract, consulting agreement, speaking engagement, or contractor agreement does not constitute a transfer, assignment, license, sale, or surrender of any proprietary intellectual property, business models, or trademarks. No contracting entity, school, agency, partner, or independent contractor acquires any ownership, equity, or derivative rights whatsoever in the independent brand assets of Tiffany A. Davidson or WISECEO.

    3. Strict Limitation of Contractor and Partner Scope

    Any independent teacher, contractor, guest speaker, or partner educator operating under an agreement with Tiffany A. Davidson or WISECEO is granted a strictly limited, non-exclusive, revocable, and non-transferable license to utilize designated instructional frameworks solely for the explicit duration and scope of their approved engagement.

    • Under no circumstances does a contractor or partner relationship transfer ownership, co-authorship, or independent distribution rights to any proprietary materials.
    • Upon the termination of any agreement, all rights to utilize, teach, or distribute proprietary frameworks immediately cease, and all materials must be returned or destroyed.

    4. Strict Limitation of Institutional Contractual Scope

    Services rendered under any institutional, educational, or governmental contract are strictly limited to the specific, agreed-upon scope of those individual contracts. Performance of teaching duties, professional development facilitation, keynote speaking, or advisory consulting does not incorporate, license, or merge private proprietary frameworks into the contracting agency or institution. All independent commercial and educational enterprises remain strictly separate from institutional work.

    5. Reservation of All Rights and Enforcement

    All intellectual property and commercial rights are explicitly reserved. Unauthorized use, reproduction, commercial exploitation, sub-licensing, or claimed ownership of the WISECEO brand or associated proprietary frameworks by any current or past contracting partner, employer, agency, employee, or contractor is strictly prohibited and will be prosecuted to the fullest extent of the law.

    6. Official Inquiries

    For formal verification of intellectual property ownership or to request authorized licensing inquiries regarding institutional partnerships or approved contractor programs, please contact us directly through our official portal at wiseceo.org.

    Licensing & verification inquiries.

    To verify intellectual property ownership or ask about licensing for institutional partnerships or approved contractor programs, contact us directly.
