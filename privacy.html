Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

Last updated: October 2026

W.I.S.E.C.E.O. (Worldwide Institute for Success & Excellence) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization based in West Chester, Ohio (EIN 41-3404888). This policy explains what information we collect through www.wiseceo.org, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, area of interest, and message. Messages are delivered to us by our form service, Web3Forms.
  • Donations: donations are processed securely by Donorbox and its payment processors. We receive your name, email, donation amount, and any information you choose to share. We never see or store your full card or bank details.
  • Newsletter: if you subscribe, your email address is stored by our newsletter provider, beehiiv.
  • Basic technical data: our hosting provider, Vercel, may log standard information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited to keep the site secure and working.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions and requests
  • To process donations and send tax receipts
  • To send our newsletter and program updates if you subscribe
  • To keep our website secure and improve it

What we don’t do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not show advertising on this website. We share information only with the service providers listed above, to do the work you asked for, or when required by law.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the link in any email.
  • Ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for adults — families, educators, donors, and partners. We do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Our website uses HTTPS encryption, and we rely on reputable providers to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
