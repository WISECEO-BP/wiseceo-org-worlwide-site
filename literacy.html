Literacy & Language Access

Literacy is the first door
to opportunity.

Every career pathway — healthcare, advanced manufacturing, the skilled trades, technology — begins with the ability to read, write, and communicate with confidence. For English language learners, immigrant and refugee families, first-generation students, and students with disabilities, that foundation is too often left to chance.

W.I.S.E.C.E.O.’s literacy and language work brings research-based reading and language instruction to the learners and families who need it most, and equips the educators who serve them. It is the heart of our Education Access & Equity program — and the foundation under every workforce pathway we build.
