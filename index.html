    Creating Equity & Opportunity.

    For every community. Everywhere.

    W.I.S.E.C.E.O. bridges the digital divide and expands access to technology, workforce readiness, and economic mobility for historically underserved communities worldwide.

    Our Impact

    Three decades in. 25,000 students reached.

    W.I.S.E.C.E.O. is built by educators with deep roots in public education. Across our chapters, every one led by a partner teacher and administrator, we have reached more than 25,000 students — and that experience is the foundation for everything we're building next.
