Our Story

Excellence is not a privilege.
It's a right.

W.I.S.E.C.E.O. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to dismantling systemic barriers that prevent underserved communities from accessing technology, education, and economic opportunity. We serve first-generation learners, immigrants, refugees, and low-income youth and adults — communities historically underrepresented in technology and the workforce.

Founded and led by educators with more than 30 years in the public school system, we've reached over 1,000 international students to date. Through coding education, digital literacy, career pathway development, and entrepreneurship support, we equip individuals with the critical skills needed to succeed in today's innovation economy — generating measurable, lasting economic impact.

Meet Our Founder

Tiffany Davidson, M.Ed.

Tiffany Davidson is an educator, literacy coach, and language-acquisition specialist who founded W.I.S.E.C.E.O. to open the doors that education opened for her. She holds two master’s degrees — in Literacy and Language Acquisition, and in Curriculum & Instruction — along with certifications in TESOL and English Language Arts, and training in gifted and Section 504 services and administrative leadership.

Her work centers on the learners schools too often overlook: English language learners, immigrant and refugee families, first-generation students, and students with IEPs. As a literacy coach she helps teachers make every lesson accessible through research-based approaches such as SIOP and differentiated instruction, and she helps families understand and use their rights so their children get the support they deserve.

At W.I.S.E.C.E.O., that experience powers our belief that literacy is the first job skill — and the foundation of economic mobility.
