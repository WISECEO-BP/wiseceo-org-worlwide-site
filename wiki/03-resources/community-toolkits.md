# Community Toolkits & Educational Materials

These are W.I.S.E.C.E.O.'s free, published guides for families and educators. Each one is a full article on wiseceo.org that you can read, share or print.

## Which guides are for families?

Two guides help families support learning and advocate at school:

- [Your Child's IEP, Explained: A Family Guide to the Meeting Table](https://www.wiseceo.org/articles/iep-family-guide.html): what an IEP is, your rights, and the questions to bring, especially if your family speaks a language other than English at home.
- [Reading Is the First Job Skill: Why Literacy Is Workforce Development](https://www.wiseceo.org/articles/reading-is-the-first-job-skill.html): how families can build reading at home, at any age.

## Which guides are for educators?

One guide gives teachers practical strategies for English learners.

- [Six SIOP Moves Every Teacher Can Use Tomorrow With English Learners](https://www.wiseceo.org/articles/siop-moves-for-teachers.html): practical, research-based strategies, no new curriculum required.

## Which guides cover civic and policy topics?

Two articles explain policy issues that affect the families we serve.

- [Special Caucus Edition: Whose Voices Shaped This Election?](https://www.wiseceo.org/listen/2026-election.html): what recent election firsts mean for language access, English learners and multilingual communities.
- [Taxing the Unbanked: Why Ohio Should Protect Check-Cashing and Payday Borrowers](https://www.wiseceo.org/articles/taxing-the-unbanked.html): Ohio reform history and the protections every borrower can use.

## How do I get new guides?

[Subscribe to the newsletter](https://www.wiseceo.org/newsletter.html) to receive new guides as they are published.
