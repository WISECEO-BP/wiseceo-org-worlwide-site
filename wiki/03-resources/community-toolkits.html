Community Toolkits & Educational Materials

From the W.I.S.E.C.E.O. Knowledge Hub

These are W.I.S.E.C.E.O.'s free, published guides for families and educators. Each one is a full article on wiseceo.org that you can read, share or print.

Which guides are for families?

Two guides help families support learning and advocate at school:

  • Your Child's IEP, Explained: A Family Guide to the Meeting Table: what an IEP is, your rights, and the questions to bring, especially if your family speaks a language other than English at home.
  • Reading Is the First Job Skill: Why Literacy Is Workforce Development: how families can build reading at home, at any age.

Which guides are for educators?

  • Six SIOP Moves Every Teacher Can Use Tomorrow With English Learners: practical, research-based strategies, no new curriculum required.

Which guides cover civic and policy topics?

  • Special Caucus Edition: Whose Voices Shaped This Election?: what recent election firsts mean for language access, English learners and multilingual communities.
  • Taxing the Unbanked: Why Ohio Should Protect Check-Cashing and Payday Borrowers: Ohio reform history and the protections every borrower can use.

How do I get new guides?

Subscribe to the newsletter to receive new guides as they are published.
