Worldwide Institute for Success and Excellence (W.I.S.E.C.E.O.)

Worldwide Institute for Success and Excellence (W.I.S.E.C.E.O.) is an Ohio-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to advancing digital literacy, workforce development, and economic mobility.

Overview

Founded by Tiffany Davidson, M.Ed. LSLS, M.Ed. C&I, W.I.S.E.C.E.O. bridges professional expertise with community-empowering frameworks, focusing on sustainable educational and economic equity. It has reached more than 25,000 students, with learners from 35 nations. See the organizational overview.

Core Initiatives

W.I.S.E.C.E.O.'s work falls into three areas, each with its own page:

  • Digital Literacy Programs: Bridging the digital divide by providing accessible technology training and essential digital skills.
  • Workforce Development: Expanding workforce readiness through structured programs, professional development, and practical training modules.
  • Economic Mobility: Driving sustainable economic mobility initiatives to create opportunity and equity for individuals and communities.

Governance & Leadership

W.I.S.E.C.E.O. operates as a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit entity in the state of Ohio, headquartered in West Chester, Ohio, with chapters in Kentucky, Florida and Mississippi. Executive leadership is directed by founder Tiffany Davidson, M.Ed. LSLS, M.Ed. C&I.
