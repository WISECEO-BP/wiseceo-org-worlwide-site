Worldwide Institute for Success and Excellence (W.I.S.E.C.E.O.)

From the W.I.S.E.C.E.O. Knowledge Hub

Worldwide Institute for Success and Excellence (W.I.S.E.C.E.O.) is an Ohio-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, headquartered in West Chester in Southwest Ohio, dedicated to advancing digital literacy, workforce development, and economic mobility.

Overview

Worldwide Institute for Success and Excellence (W.I.S.E.C.E.O.) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded by Tiffany Davidson, M.Ed. LSLS, M.Ed. C&I, that advances digital literacy, workforce development and economic mobility for underserved learners. It has served more than 25,000 learners and community members, with learners from 35 nations.

W.I.S.E.C.E.O. bridges professional expertise with community-empowering frameworks, focusing on sustainable educational and economic equity. See the organizational overview.

Core Initiatives

W.I.S.E.C.E.O.'s three core initiatives are digital literacy, workforce development and economic mobility, each designed for first-generation, immigrant, refugee and low-income learners. Each has its own page:

  • Digital Literacy Programs: Bridging the digital divide by providing accessible technology training and essential digital skills.
  • Workforce Development: Expanding workforce readiness through structured programs, professional development, and practical training modules.
  • Economic Mobility: Driving sustainable economic mobility initiatives to create opportunity and equity for individuals and communities.

Governance & Leadership

Worldwide Institute for Success and Excellence (W.I.S.E.C.E.O.) is a registered Ohio 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 41-3404888) headquartered in West Chester, Ohio, and led by founder Tiffany Davidson, M.Ed. LSLS, M.Ed. C&I. It also has chapters in Kentucky, Florida and Mississippi.
