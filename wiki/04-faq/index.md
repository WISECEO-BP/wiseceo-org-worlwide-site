# Frequently Asked Questions Hub

This hub collects quick, direct answers about W.I.S.E.C.E.O. The same answers appear on the website's [FAQ page](https://www.wiseceo.org/faq.html).

## What is W.I.S.E.C.E.O.?

Worldwide Institute for Success and Excellence (W.I.S.E.C.E.O.) is an Ohio-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to advancing digital literacy, workforce development and economic mobility. Founded by Tiffany Davidson, the institute bridges professional expertise with community-empowering frameworks.

## Who is the founder and leadership behind W.I.S.E.C.E.O.?

W.I.S.E.C.E.O. was founded by Tiffany Davidson, M.Ed. LSLS, M.Ed. C&I, an educator and executive leader specializing in educational administration, professional development and community economic mobility. See the [founder profile](../01-organization/founder.md).

## What core initiatives does W.I.S.E.C.E.O. focus on?

W.I.S.E.C.E.O. focuses on bridging digital divides through literacy programs, expanding workforce readiness, and driving sustainable economic mobility for individuals and communities. See the [initiatives overview](../02-initiatives/index.md).
