# General Inquiries & Partnerships

The fastest way to reach W.I.S.E.C.E.O. is the [contact form](https://www.wiseceo.org/contact.html) or admin@wiseceo.org. This page answers the most common "how do I…" questions.

## How do I contact W.I.S.E.C.E.O.?

Use the [contact form](https://www.wiseceo.org/contact.html) or email admin@wiseceo.org. Mail goes to 7240 Towne Centre Blvd, Suite 78, West Chester, OH 45069.

## How do I apply to a program?

Each program has its own application on the [Programs page](https://www.wiseceo.org/programs.html). Applying never signs you up for marketing emails.

## How can my organization partner with W.I.S.E.C.E.O.?

Organizations can partner on Entrepreneurship & Innovation or Community Economic Resilience through the [application page](https://www.wiseceo.org/apply.html), or [contact us](https://www.wiseceo.org/contact.html) to discuss other collaborations.

## How do I start a chapter or volunteer?

[Contact us](https://www.wiseceo.org/contact.html) and choose "Volunteering" or "Partnering / Collaborating." Chapters are led by a partner teacher and administrator.

## How do I give?

Give online or directly by check or bank transfer on the [Give page](https://www.wiseceo.org/give.html). Gifts are tax-deductible; the EIN is 41-3404888.

## Do you offer services in other languages?

Yes. Language access, including interpretation and translation, is part of every program, and the website can be read in several languages from its language menu.
