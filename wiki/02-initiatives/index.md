# Initiatives Overview

W.I.S.E.C.E.O.'s initiatives fall into three areas: digital literacy, workforce development and economic mobility. Every program pairs instruction with wrap-around support so learners can finish what they start.

## What programs does W.I.S.E.C.E.O. run?

The current programs are listed on the [Programs page](https://www.wiseceo.org/programs.html). Each one belongs to one of the three areas below.

| Program | Area |

| Digital Literacy & Coding | [Digital literacy](digital-literacy.md) |
| Literacy & Language Access | [Digital literacy](digital-literacy.md) |
| Workforce Development | [Workforce development](workforce-development.md) |
| Youth Career & Tech Initiative | [Workforce development](workforce-development.md) |
| Education Access & Equity | [Economic mobility](economic-mobility.md) |
| Entrepreneurship & Innovation | [Economic mobility](economic-mobility.md) |
| Community Economic Resilience | [Economic mobility](economic-mobility.md) |

## What support comes with every program?

Every learner can get four kinds of wrap-around support:

- **Language access:** interpretation, translation and multilingual support for learners and families.
- **Mentoring and coaching:** regular check-ins, tutoring and mentors.
- **Devices and connectivity:** help getting laptops and internet access, plus digital-literacy onboarding.
- **Career services:** resume help, interview preparation, job readiness and employer connections.
