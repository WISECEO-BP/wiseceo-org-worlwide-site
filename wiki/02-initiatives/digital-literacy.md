# Digital Literacy Programs & Frameworks

W.I.S.E.C.E.O.'s digital literacy work teaches coding, reading and digital skills to communities historically left behind. It rests on one belief: literacy is the first job skill, and the foundation of economic mobility.

## What is Digital Literacy & Coding?

Digital Literacy & Coding brings coding, creativity and technology education to communities historically left behind, through app design, creative problem-solving and hands-on learning. Learners can [join the waitlist](https://www.wiseceo.org/apply.html?program=digital-literacy).

## What is Literacy & Language Access?

Literacy & Language Access brings research-based reading and language instruction to learners and families who need it most, and equips the educators who serve them. It is the heart of the Education Access & Equity program.

It has five strands:

- **Family literacy:** guides and gatherings that help caregivers build reading habits at home, in English or their home language.
- **English learner support:** SIOP-informed strategies and academic-language development for multilingual and newcomer students.
- **Family advocacy and IEP navigation:** plain-language workshops on special education, IEPs and 504 plans, including for families who speak a language other than English.
- **Educator learning:** differentiation, SIOP and literacy across the content areas for teachers and paraprofessionals.
- **Career and digital literacy:** technical vocabulary, job applications, workplace communication and the digital skills employers expect.

## What approaches does the work use?

The work uses research-based approaches, chiefly SIOP (the Sheltered Instruction Observation Protocol) and differentiated instruction. In practice that means language objectives, visuals and sentence frames that make lessons accessible to every learner.

## Who leads it?

The literacy work is led by founder Tiffany Davidson, M.Ed. LSLS, M.Ed. C&I, who holds master's degrees in Literacy & Language Acquisition and in Curriculum & Instruction, plus TESOL certification.
