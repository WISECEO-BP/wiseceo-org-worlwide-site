Workforce Readiness & Training

From the W.I.S.E.C.E.O. Knowledge Hub

W.I.S.E.C.E.O.'s workforce programs connect learners to in-demand, high-wage careers through sector-based training and youth career and technology programs.

What is Workforce Development?

Workforce Development is sector-based training that aligns participants with in-demand, high-wage careers and connects them to competitive employers. Learners can join the waitlist.

What is the Youth Career & Tech Initiative?

The Youth Career & Tech Initiative offers out-of-school career exploration, technical skill-building and community mentorship that help young people build practical readiness and economic mobility. Young people can join the interest list.

Is WISECEO Academy part of W.I.S.E.C.E.O.?

No. WISECEO Academy (wiseceoacademy.com) is a separate for-profit enterprise that runs its own career fellowships and paid training. W.I.S.E.C.E.O. programs are listed on the Programs page.

Which partners do learners work with?

Students we teach and mentor take part in programs led by national, regional and civic organizations:

  • Amazon Future Engineer: Amazon's childhood-to-career computer science and STEM program.
  • AWS Community Engagement: technology education, volunteers and career exposure for local learners.
  • TechOlympics: the nation's largest student-run technology conference, in Greater Cincinnati.
  • DC Department of Employment Services: career pathways, technical training and apprenticeships.
  • DC Council and civic leadership: youth development policy advocacy through the Jack and Jill of America On the Hill Legislative Summit.

What career services are included?

Every workforce learner can get resume help, interview preparation, job readiness training and connections to employers.
