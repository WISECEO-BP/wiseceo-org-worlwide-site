# Economic Mobility & Community Equity Projects

W.I.S.E.C.E.O.'s economic mobility work widens access to education, supports small businesses, and builds lasting capacity in economically distressed communities.

## What is Education Access & Equity?

Education Access & Equity expands access to quality education for first-generation students, English language learners, immigrants and refugees, building pathways to economic mobility.

Its literacy and language strand is described in [Digital literacy](digital-literacy.md). Learners can [join the interest list](https://www.wiseceo.org/apply.html?program=education-access).

## What is Entrepreneurship & Innovation?

Entrepreneurship & Innovation supports small business development, startup incubation and innovation ecosystems in distressed communities, creating jobs and attracting private investment. Organizations can [partner with us](https://www.wiseceo.org/apply.html?program=entrepreneurship).

## What is Community Economic Resilience?

Community Economic Resilience strengthens economically distressed communities through technical assistance, strategic planning and investments that build lasting capacity for growth. Organizations can [partner with us](https://www.wiseceo.org/apply.html?program=community-resilience).

## What impact has W.I.S.E.C.E.O. had?

W.I.S.E.C.E.O. has served more than 25,000 learners and community members, about 2,500 a year, with learners from 35 nations across 10 states plus Seattle.

## How can a donation help?

On the giving page, $25 puts a coding starter kit in one first-time learner's hands, $50 sponsors a week of digital-literacy training, and $250 funds a full workforce-readiness pathway. See [Give](https://www.wiseceo.org/give.html).
