Founder Profile — Tiffany Davidson

From the W.I.S.E.C.E.O. Knowledge Hub

Tiffany Davidson is the founder of W.I.S.E.C.E.O. She is an educator, literacy advocate and language-acquisition specialist who founded the organization to open the doors that education opened for her.

What is her educational background?

She holds two master's degrees and a bachelor's degree, plus certifications in TESOL and English Language Arts:

  • M.Ed. in Literacy and Language Acquisition, University of Cincinnati
  • M.Ed. in Curriculum & Instruction, University of Southern Mississippi
  • B.S. in English, University of Southern Mississippi
  • Training in gifted and Section 504 services and in administrative leadership

What is her professional experience?

Her classroom work spans high schools in Ohio, Florida and Mississippi, and she spent more than a decade in business before teaching.

As ESL department chair and STEAM Club advisor at Western Hills High School in Cincinnati, she taught 150 multilingual students and mentored student STEM projects in partnership with TechOlympics and Amazon engineers. Before returning to the classroom, she spent more than a decade in business development, marketing and financial services, experience she now brings to financial literacy education.

Whom does her work focus on?

Her work centers on learners schools too often overlook: English language learners, immigrant and refugee families, first-generation students, and students with IEPs. She helps teachers make lessons accessible through research-based approaches such as SIOP and differentiated instruction, and helps families understand and use their rights.

Recognition and affiliations

She is listed in Marquis Who's Who in America (2026–2027) and holds the Certified Scrum Master (CSM) credential.

  • TESOL International Association (2024 Conference)
  • Ohio TESOL (2024 Conference)
  • National Council of Teachers of English (NCTE)
  • PDK International
  • CFT-AFT

Source: Tiffany Davidson, Founder
