Brand Standards & Official Terminology

From the W.I.S.E.C.E.O. Knowledge Hub

The organization's official name is written W.I.S.E.C.E.O., with periods, and its legal name is Worldwide Institute For Success And Excellence. The registered trademark form without periods is WISECEO®.

How should the name be written?

Use "W.I.S.E.C.E.O." in running text and "Worldwide Institute For Success And Excellence (W.I.S.E.C.E.O.)" on first mention in formal documents.

What do the letters stand for?

The letters spell out the organization's name and its promise: Worldwide Institute (for) Success (and) Excellence — Creating Equity (&) Opportunity.

What is the tagline?

The tagline is "Creating Equity & Opportunity," often followed by "For every community. Everywhere."

What are the core focus areas?

The homepage names five: Digital Access & Equity, Workforce Development, Innovation & Entrepreneurship, Economic Mobility, and Community Resilience.

Mascot

The WISECEO Academy mascot is Opa Homakbi, an owl whose name is Choctaw for "Purple Owl." She helps learners pick a program track.
