Governance & Legal Status

From the W.I.S.E.C.E.O. Knowledge Hub

W.I.S.E.C.E.O. operates as Worldwide Institute For Success And Excellence, a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization with EIN 41-3404888. Donations are tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law.

How do WISECEO and WISECEO Academy relate?

WISECEO, the 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and WISECEO Academy (wiseceoacademy.com), a separate for-profit enterprise, are legally and administratively distinct entities. Both license foundational instructional frameworks owned by Tiffany Davidson.

Who owns the brand and curricula?

The WISECEO trademarks, curricula and frameworks are the private property of Tiffany A. Davidson and are protected under U.S. and international copyright law.

The full terms are in the Legal & IP disclaimer and IP policy.
