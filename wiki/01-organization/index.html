Organizational Overview & Mission

W.I.S.E.C.E.O. (Worldwide Institute For Success And Excellence) is an independent, community-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit that dismantles systemic barriers to technology, education and economic opportunity for underserved communities. It was founded and is led by educators with more than 30 years in the public school system.

What is the mission?

Our mission is to dismantle systemic barriers to technology, education and economic opportunity by giving underserved learners the skills, credentials and wrap-around support they need to build careers and lasting economic mobility.

What is the vision?

We envision a world where every learner, whatever their language, origin or zip code, has a real path to meaningful work, financial stability and leadership in their community.

Who does W.I.S.E.C.E.O. serve?

We serve learners historically underrepresented in technology and the workforce. That includes:

  • Refugees and immigrants, including newcomer families and English learners
  • Underserved students, high school age and older
  • Underserved women and girls
  • Veterans
  • First-generation and low-income youth and adults seeking economic mobility

Where does W.I.S.E.C.E.O. work?

We are headquartered in West Chester, Ohio, with chapters in Kentucky, Florida and Mississippi. Our impact also reaches Georgia, Michigan, New York, Indiana, Oklahoma, Tennessee and Seattle, Washington, with learners from 35 nations.

How are programs delivered?

Programs are run by dedicated program staff, supported by trained volunteers and chapter leads, and delivered through our chapters and partner organizations. That network lets us enroll and support cohorts of 250 or more learners at a time. W.I.S.E.C.E.O. is not a school, a government agency or a religious organization.
